# Awesome-Chocolate-Power-BI-Dashboard

Awesome Chocolate Sales Dashboard
Overview:
This Power BI dashboard analyzes sales, profit, shipment, and product performance for a chocolate business between January 2023 and February 2025. The report provides in-depth insights into total sales amount, profit margins, shipments, geographic distribution, top products, and top sales personnel.
Project Features:
	•	Total sales analysis: $141.49M revenue, $81.1M profit (57.3% margin), 9M boxes sold, 25K shipments.
	•	Year-on-year comparison: Visual comparison of current year vs previous year trends for amount and boxes sold.
	•	Shipments distribution: Histogram showing shipment counts across box categories.
	•	Product breakdown: Profitability and revenue for each product in the portfolio.
	•	Geo analysis: Pie chart of sales revenue by country/region.
	•	Top sales performers: Individual breakdown for 5 leading salespersons (revenue, boxes, profit percentage).
	•	Dynamic calendar filter: Quickly filter results by custom date ranges.
Key Insights:
	•	The highest sales come from India and New Zealand, representing over 50% of revenue.
	•	Top-selling products include Organic Choco Syrup, Caramel Stuffed Bars, and 99% Dark & Pure Chocolate.
	•	Product-level profitability ranges from 36% to 33%, with only a few products below 10% margin.
	•	The best-performing salesperson is Ponnan ($11.3M sales, 58.8% profit).
